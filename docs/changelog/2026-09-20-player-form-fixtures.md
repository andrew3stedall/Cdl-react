# 2026-09-20 — Player fixture form dots

Player cards now show the player’s latest five completed FPL gameweeks as five
form circles. Each appearance is coloured on the 0–8+ points scale, an
appearance with no minutes remains grey, and a double gameweek is rendered as
two half-colours in one circle. The history is sourced from the cached official
FPL element-summary and event-live payloads, and remains explicitly grey when
that official history is unavailable.
